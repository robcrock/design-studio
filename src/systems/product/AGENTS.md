# Product system instructions

This system's components, theme, context and skills support prototypes assigned to it. Out of the box it is the app system for Echo, Design Studio's example product. Follow the repository's platform working context.

Before relevant work, read [Echo brand](context/brand.md) and [Echo product](context/product.md), then any applicable skills. When you replace Echo with your own product, replace these documents too. Empty sections do not require invented personas, principles, or procedures.
