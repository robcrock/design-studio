Echo is a made-up B2B product used as Design Studio's example. It collects customer feedback from email, chat, calls, and surveys, ties each request to the account behind it, and helps product teams decide what to build next.

Product and [Marketing](/systems/v4576ka1mbpbqdp1/context/brand) share one brand and keep separate systems, the way most in-house teams do: the app is dense and quiet, the website is bold and spacious.

## Shared foundations

- **Name and mark:** Echo. The mark is a dot with two widening arcs, a sound and its echo, drawn in a single color.
- **Color:** cobalt `#2F5BFF` is the one brand color. Everything else is zinc neutrals with white surfaces.
- **Type:** Geist for text, Geist Mono for numbers, IDs, and code. No other typefaces.
- **Voice:** plain, specific, and calm. Short sentences, real numbers, no hype.

## In the app

- Cobalt marks the primary action, the current place, and data. It is never a page background.
- Corners are soft (6 to 14px). Cards and dialogs use the larger radius; controls the smaller.
- Show the account and its monthly revenue beside customer feedback wherever it helps a decision.
- Light and dark modes are both first-class.
