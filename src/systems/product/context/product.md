## Who it's for

Product teams at B2B software companies. The sample data follows one such team:

- **Sam Rivera, product lead:** triages new feedback and decides what goes on the roadmap.
- **Support leads and account managers:** capture what customers say, quickly, from wherever they hear it.
- **Designers:** look for patterns worth exploring.

## Objects and terms

- **Feedback:** one thing a customer told us. It has a title, the customer's words, a source, a priority, and a status.
- **Customer** and **account:** the person who said it, and the company they belong to. An account has a plan and monthly revenue (MRR).
- **Source:** Email, Chat, Interview, or Survey.
- **Status:** New, Triaged, Planned, or Resolved, in that order.
- **Views:** saved filters in the sidebar, such as High priority or Needs triage.

Use these words in the interface. Say "feedback", not "tickets"; say "account", not "client".
