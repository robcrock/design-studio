---
title: Marketing design
description: Composition and copy conventions for Echo's website.
---

- Lead with the product. The hero shows a real Echo screen, recreated in this prototype's code, right under a short headline.
- Give each section one clear purpose and one idea. Keep only the copy that supports it.
- Use one accent. Cobalt carries emphasis; everything else stays neutral.
- Do not use eyebrow labels or small all-caps text. Use sentence-case headings and ordinary body text.
- Separate sections with whitespace or a change of background, not decorative divider lines.
- Echo, its customers, and its numbers are fictional. Never use real company names or logos as customers.
- Keep marketing projects self-contained. Recreate product screens here rather than linking visitors into the app prototype.
- Use `buttonVariants` on plain anchors for links styled as buttons, so they stay links.
