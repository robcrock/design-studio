## Purpose

Echo is a made-up product: one inbox for customer feedback, with every request tied to the account and revenue behind it. This prototype is its website. It sits beside the [Echo app](/prototypes/ccncfpcyaa1hvjts/artifacts/0qd3s4spbfcpbb2j), the way an in-house team keeps a product and its marketing site in step.

The homepage has one job: show a product team what Echo does in a few seconds, then get them to start free.

## System

This prototype uses **Marketing**. It shares Echo's brand with the app's **Product** system (the mark, the cobalt, and Geist type) and adds what a website needs: larger type, a brand-colour hero, and room to breathe. Follow the [Marketing design rule](/systems/v4576ka1mbpbqdp1/context/design).

The product shot in the hero is the app's inbox recreated in markup, so it stays sharp at any size and follows the theme.

## What to review

Start on the [Review Board](review-board.excalidraw).

- [Home](home.tsx) is the final page. Check it on desktop and phone, in light and dark. Its hero headline size and how far the product shot hangs below the hero are DialKit dials.
- [Alternative light hero](alternative-light-hero.tsx) is the same page with cobalt only in the headline and buttons. It's kept as an example of comparing iterations side by side.

Customers, quotes, and figures on the page are fictional. It uses no real logos or company names.
